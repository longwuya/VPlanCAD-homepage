Abstract

Engineering drawings and parametric CAD models constitute complementary carriers of product knowledge across the engineering lifecycle. While drawings encode projection-based geometric information, parametric CAD models preserve editable construction logic, feature dependencies, and modeling histories. Transforming two-dimensional drawings into such models is therefore not merely a geometric reconstruction task, but an engineering information conversion problem that links legacy documentation with digital design, analysis, and manufacturing workflows. Existing drawing-driven approaches commonly formulate this task as direct sequence translation, which limits their ability to organize complementary multi-view information, represent geometry-dependent local operations, and maintain consistency across the construction process. This paper presents VPlanCAD, a view-aware and planning-guided framework for editable parametric CAD reconstruction. Rather than collapsing all views into a single undifferentiated sequence, VPlanCAD retains view-specific semantics and integrates complementary geometric cues through view-wise aggregation and cross-view interaction. The fused representation is then transformed into a command-level construction plan that guides the joint prediction of CAD operations, target entities, and parameters, allowing basic modeling steps and local boundary refinements to be organized within a coherent executable history. A unified learning objective couples planning, command, and parameter prediction. Experiments under single- and multi-view settings show that VPlanCAD improves reconstruction accuracy, sequence validity, model executability, and geometric fidelity over representative baselines.

Introduction

In this work, we propose VPlanCAD, a planning-aware multi-view framework for generating editable parametric CAD histories from vector engineering drawings. The framework formulates CAD generation as a structured transformation jointly constrained by multi-view geometric information and dependencies among modeling operations.

Ablation

The ablation analysis examines the two principal components of VPlanCAD at the representation and generation levels.
